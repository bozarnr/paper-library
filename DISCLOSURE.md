# Disclosure Boundary

This repository publishes bounded replication evidence, not a private research archive.

## Included

- Human-readable replication cards with source, scope, observed evidence, deviations, and verdicts.
- Machine-readable metadata cards for claim ceilings and evidence links.
- A synthetic evidence matrix sample for schema inspection.

## Excluded

- Raw notebooks, private logs, private datasets, complete factor formulas, credentials, and vendor data.
- Paper text beyond short references and original commentary.
- Any claim that a partial implementation is a strict paper replication or a deployable strategy.

## Sample Data Rule

Files under `sample_data/` show public metadata format only. They are not research results.
